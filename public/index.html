Every figure on this page is derived, not typed. Concentrations come from raw counts multiplied by a conversion factor computed per site and per week from measured flow rate and traverse geometry, and are never rounded before aggregation. Gaps are shown as gaps: a day with no valid sample is excluded from means and marked on the tape, rather than being read as zero.
Risk bands are placeholders and must be set by the network's clinical advisory group before public release. Season descriptors use the 95% cumulative method (2.5% to 97.5% of the annual integral).
